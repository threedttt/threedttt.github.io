Wireless Scanner 隱私權政策

本隱私權政策說明由 PLAYNJOIN Team （以下稱「我們」）提供的 Wireless Scanner Android 應用程式 （以下稱「本應用程式」）如何存取、使用、儲存及分享資料。

Wireless Scanner 不要求使用者建立帳戶，也沒有將掃描紀錄或配對資料 上傳至 PLAYNJOIN Team 自有伺服器。 本應用程式使用 Google Mobile Ads 和 Google Play Billing， 這些 Google 服務可能依其政策處理部分資料。

1. 相機與條碼掃描

本應用程式需要相機權限，以辨識條碼及 QR Code。 相機畫面只會在裝置上即時處理；本應用程式不會儲存相片或影片， 也不會將相機影像傳送給 PLAYNJOIN Team。

2. 掃描內容與本機紀錄

掃描或手動輸入的條碼文字可能儲存在裝置上的本機資料庫，以提供：

  • 掃描歷史紀錄；
  • 顯示先前掃描內容；
  • 重新傳送掃描內容到使用者配對的電腦。

掃描內容由使用者決定，可能包含使用者自行輸入或掃描的資料。 PLAYNJOIN Team 無法從遠端查看這些本機紀錄。

使用者可以在應用程式內清除掃描紀錄，也可以透過 Android 系統清除 應用程式資料或解除安裝本應用程式。

3. 與電腦配對及資料傳輸

使用者可以將 Wireless Scanner 與自己控制的 Windows 或 macOS 電腦配對。當使用者選擇傳送或重新傳送掃描內容時，本應用程式會透過 區域網路，將資料傳送到使用者已配對的電腦。

配對傳輸使用經驗證及加密的通訊協定。PLAYNJOIN Team 不會接收、 代理或儲存這些掃描內容。

裝置上可能保存以下配對資訊：

  • 裝置及配對伺服器識別資訊；
  • 加密的配對憑證；
  • 已配對電腦的區域網路位址及連接埠；
  • 維持安全傳輸所需的通訊序號。

配對密鑰使用 Android Keystore 保護。配對憑證、裝置識別資訊、 安全序號及已配對端點已排除在 Android 雲端備份及裝置移轉之外。

4. 應用程式設定與 Android 備份

本應用程式會在裝置上保存語言、外觀、掃描按鈕、進階功能設定、 使用量狀態及本機購買權益快取等設定。

一般設定與掃描歷史紀錄可能依使用者的 Android 系統設定， 由 Android 備份服務納入雲端備份或裝置移轉。 備份資料的保存及安全性由 Android、Google、裝置製造商及使用者的 備份設定管理。

配對憑證及已配對電腦端點不包含在本應用程式允許的備份資料中。

5. 廣告及 Google Mobile Ads

免費版可能顯示橫幅廣告及獎勵廣告。本應用程式使用 Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）。依 Google 的公開說明，Mobile Ads SDK 可能自動收集及分享以下資料：

  • IP 位址，可能用於推算大概位置；
  • 應用程式互動，例如啟動、點擊及廣告影片觀看；
  • 診斷資訊，例如啟動時間、效能及 SDK 運作資訊；
  • 裝置或其他識別碼，例如 Android 廣告 ID、App Set ID。

這些資料可能用於：

  • 顯示、提供及衡量廣告；
  • 數據分析及服務效能改善；
  • 詐欺防範、安全性及法規遵循。

Google 表示 Mobile Ads SDK 傳輸的資料使用 TLS 加密。 Google 及其廣告合作夥伴會依各自的隱私權政策處理資料。

在歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士或其他適用地區，本應用程式使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）提供同意及隱私權選項。 使用者可以在應用程式選單中使用「隱私權選項」 （該選項在適用地區及需要時顯示）重新檢視或調整選擇。

即使使用者不同意個人化廣告，Google 仍可能基於受限廣告、 網路通訊、詐欺防範、安全性或法規要求處理部分資料。 部分廣告或獎勵廣告可能因此無法提供。

Google 相關資訊：

  • Google 隱私權政策
  • Google 如何使用廣告資料
  • Google Mobile Ads Data Safety 揭露

6. Google Play Billing 與購買

Wireless Scanner 提供由 Google Play Billing 處理的一次性應用程式內購買， 例如移除廣告及解鎖進階功能。

付款方式、信用卡資料及交易處理由 Google Play 負責。 Wireless Scanner 不會取得或儲存使用者的完整付款卡資料。

本應用程式會從 Google Play 取得確認購買、確認交易及恢復權益所需的 商品識別碼、購買狀態及購買權杖等資訊，並在裝置上保存本機權益狀態及 最近同步時間。本應用程式目前不會將購買權杖傳送到 PLAYNJOIN Team 自有後端。

如果購買遭退款、撤銷或不再由 Google Play 回報為有效， 本應用程式可能在下一次線上同步時移除相關權益並恢復廣告。

7. 聯絡資料

如果使用者主動透過電子郵件聯絡我們，我們會收到其電子郵件地址、 郵件內容及使用者自行提供的資訊。這些資料只用於回覆問題、 提供支援、處理法律要求或維護服務安全。

支援郵件會依處理問題及履行法律義務所需的期間保存， 之後刪除或依法律要求保留。

8. 資料保存與刪除

  • 掃描紀錄會保留在裝置上，直到使用者在應用程式中清除、 清除應用程式資料或解除安裝。
  • 一般設定會保留到使用者變更、清除應用程式資料或解除安裝。
  • 配對資料會保留到重新配對、清除應用程式資料或解除安裝。
  • Android 備份中的資料依 Android、Google 及使用者帳戶的備份政策保存。
  • AdMob 及 Google Play 處理的資料依 Google 的保存及刪除政策管理。

由於 PLAYNJOIN Team 沒有保存掃描紀錄、設定或配對資料的遠端伺服器， 我們無法從遠端存取或刪除這些本機資料。 使用者可透過應用程式的清除功能、Android 的「清除資料」功能或解除安裝 來刪除裝置上的資料。

9. 資料安全

我們採取合理措施保護資料，包括 Android 應用程式沙箱、 Android Keystore、加密的電腦配對通訊及安全的第三方連線。 然而，任何儲存或傳輸方式都無法保證百分之百安全。

10. 兒童隱私

Wireless Scanner 並非專為兒童設計，也不以兒童為目標使用者。 我們不會故意收集未達適用法律規定年齡之兒童的個人資料。 如果您認為兒童向我們提供了個人資料，請透過下方電子郵件聯絡我們。

11. 使用者權利

視使用者所在地區而定，使用者可能享有存取、更正、反對處理、 限制處理或刪除個人資料等權利。

對於裝置本機資料，使用者可透過本應用程式或 Android 系統控制。 對於 Google 所控制的廣告、識別碼及交易資料， 使用者應使用 Google 帳戶、Android 廣告隱私權設定或 Google 提供的隱私權工具。

12. 本政策的變更

我們可能因應功能、SDK、法規或服務變更更新本政策。 更新後的政策會發布在本頁，並更新頁面頂端的「最後更新日期」。

Wireless Scanner Privacy Policy — English

This Privacy Policy explains how the Wireless Scanner Android application (“the App”), provided by PLAYNJOIN Team (“we,” “us,” or “our”), accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

Wireless Scanner does not require users to create an account and does not upload scan history or pairing data to servers operated by PLAYNJOIN Team. The App uses Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Billing, which may process information under Google's own policies.

1. Camera and barcode scanning

The App requests camera permission to scan barcodes and QR codes. Camera frames are processed locally and in real time. The App does not store photos or videos and does not transmit camera images to PLAYNJOIN Team.

2. Scan content and local history

Barcode text that is scanned or entered manually may be stored in a local database on the device to provide scan history, display previous scans, and resend scan content to the user's paired computer.

Scan content is determined by the user and may contain information selected or entered by the user. PLAYNJOIN Team cannot remotely access this local scan history.

Users can clear scan history in the App, clear the App's data through Android settings, or uninstall the App.

3. Computer pairing and data transfer

Users may pair Wireless Scanner with a Windows or macOS computer they control. When the user sends or resends scan content, the App transmits that content over the local network to the paired computer.

Paired transfers use an authenticated and encrypted protocol. PLAYNJOIN Team does not receive, relay, or store this scan content.

Pairing information stored on the device may include device and server identifiers, encrypted pairing credentials, the paired computer's local network address and port, and communication sequence values used to maintain secure transfers.

Pairing keys are protected using Android Keystore. Pairing credentials, device identity, security sequence values, and paired endpoint information are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

4. App settings and Android backup

The App stores settings such as language, appearance, scan-button preferences, advanced-feature settings, usage state, and a local purchase entitlement cache on the device.

General settings and scan history may be included in Android cloud backup or device transfer, depending on the user's Android backup settings. Backup retention and security are managed by Android, Google, the device manufacturer, and the user's backup configuration.

Pairing credentials and paired-computer endpoint information are excluded from the backup data allowed by the App.

5. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The free version may display banner and rewarded advertisements. The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). According to Google's published disclosures, the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • App interactions, including app launches, taps, and video views;
  • Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance;
  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and App Set ID.

These data may be used for:

  • Serving, displaying, and measuring advertising;
  • Analytics and service-performance improvement;
  • Fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance.

Google states that data transmitted by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google and its advertising partners process data under their respective privacy policies.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other applicable regions, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide consent and privacy choices. When required and available, users can reopen “Privacy options” from the App menu to review or change their choices.

If a user declines consent for personalized advertising, Google may still process some information for limited ads, network communication, fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance. Some ads or rewarded-ad features may consequently be unavailable.

Google resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

6. Google Play Billing and purchases

Wireless Scanner offers a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing, such as removing ads and unlocking advanced features.

Payment methods, payment-card details, and transaction processing are handled by Google Play. Wireless Scanner does not receive or store the user's full payment-card details.

The App receives product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens, and related information from Google Play as necessary to acknowledge purchases and restore entitlements. The App stores a local entitlement state and last synchronization time. Purchase tokens are not sent to a backend operated by PLAYNJOIN Team.

If a purchase is refunded, revoked, or is no longer reported as active by Google Play, the App may remove the entitlement and restore advertising after its next successful online synchronization.

7. Contact communications

If a user contacts us by email, we receive the email address, message content, and any information the user voluntarily provides. We use this information only to respond, provide support, address legal requests, or protect service security.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Scan history remains on the device until the user clears it, clears App data, or uninstalls the App.
  • General settings remain until changed, App data is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.
  • Pairing data remains until pairing is replaced, App data is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.
  • Android backup copies are governed by Android, Google, and the user's backup settings and retention rules.
  • Data processed by AdMob and Google Play is governed by Google's retention and deletion policies.

Because PLAYNJOIN Team does not maintain a server containing scan history, App settings, or pairing data, we cannot remotely access or delete this local data. Users can delete device data using the App's clear function, Android's “Clear data” control, or by uninstalling the App.

9. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards, including the Android application sandbox, Android Keystore, encrypted paired-computer communication, and secure third-party connections. However, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

Wireless Scanner is not designed for or directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

11. User rights and choices

Depending on their jurisdiction, users may have rights concerning access, correction, objection, restriction, or deletion of personal information.

Local device data can be controlled through the App and Android settings. Advertising identifiers and data controlled by Google can be managed through the user's Google Account, Android advertising-privacy settings, and Google's privacy tools.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when App functionality, SDKs, legal requirements, or services change. The revised policy will be posted on this page and the “Last updated” date will be changed.
